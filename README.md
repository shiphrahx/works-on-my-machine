## What's this

Generates billions of plausible-sounding excuses for why your code is broken, so you don't have to admit it was you.

## Features

- **Themes** — blame the *cosmos*, an *ancient curse*, the *process*, the *tech*, or pure *absurdity*.
- **Tone slider** — dial it from buttoned-up *corporate* to fully *unhinged*.
- **A severity meter** — that, for once, is actually earned.
- **Copy as anything** — plain text, a Slack status, a commit message, a Jira comment, or an out-of-office.
- **Shareable links** — send someone the *exact* disaster, reproduced byte-for-byte.
- **Favourites & history** — hoard your best excuses for the next incident.
- **Excuse of the day** — same fresh catastrophe for everyone, daily.
- Keyboard shortcut, respects reduced motion, and zero dependencies. Like your incident response plan.

## By the numbers

**200,611,015,150** possible excuses. That's 13 sentence templates remixing 115 subjects, 89 verbs, 70 states, 181 root causes, and 119 resolutions — enough to never repeat yourself before the heat death of the universe (which, incidentally, is one of the excuses).

## Run it

Just [open it in your browser](https://shiphrahx.github.io/works-on-my-machine/) — or download `index.html` and double-click it. Congratulations, it's in production.

> ⚠️ Not for use in real postmortems. The void already knows what you did.
